# Pluto Keyboard 0.6.0

Pluto's first stable release. The headline is two new voice models from NVIDIA, Nemotron and Parakeet, alongside Moonshine. Smart corrections and the setup tutorial were retuned for each voice model using a new test bench of 2,344 spoken dictations.

## New voice models: Nemotron and Parakeet

- **Nemotron** shows your words as you talk, like Moonshine. With the punctuation model it was the most accurate voice model on Pluto's test bench, with Moonshine Medium close behind. It's a 475 MB download and needs a recent, fast phone. On the test phone it keeps up using about half the time you talk, and the text is in about half a second after you stop.
- **Parakeet** types your words in one go when you finish speaking. It's a 135 MB download, and it writes numbers as words, so switch on Smart corrections' Numbers if you want digits.
- Both are English only, run entirely on your phone through [transcribe.cpp](https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp), and use your phone's fast cores. Get them from the [models page](https://github.com/ColinCPittman/Pluto-Keyboard/releases/tag/models) and import them like the other models.
- While voice input is open, typing checks wait, so they don't slow the voice model down.

## Punctuation for every voice model

- The punctuation model now also runs after Nemotron and Parakeet. Left alone, Nemotron left the period off the end of 24 of 44 dictations on the test phone, and both models miss question marks when your voice doesn't go up. With the punctuation model and the question-mark fix, Nemotron gets about 18% more dictations exactly right.
- A dictation that is only a greeting ("Dear team", "Hi Sarah") now ends with a comma instead of a period.

## Smart corrections

- **Works without pauses.** Voice commands, self-corrections, quoted speech and spoken numbers no longer need you to pause around them. When there's no pause, the whole sentence is judged instead, with stricter margins.
- **Numbers.** Dates ("march twelfth" becomes "March 12th"), clock times, phone numbers said digit by digit after "call", "text" or "my number", dollars and cents without "and", "four and a half" before a unit, and room, gate and flight numbers.
- **Misheard words** catches more of what Nemotron and Parakeet write, like "knew paragraph", "By two loaves", "serial" for "cereal" and "egg's" for "eggs", and needs fewer language model calls to do it.
- **Fewer wrong edits.** A time said last ("pasta tonight, actually tacos") is no longer cut by a self-correction, "scratch that" in a normal sentence stays as words, reported speech like "mom said I'm grounded" doesn't get quotation marks, and long digit runs are kept whole.
- **Every fix is tested for every voice model.** Each fix was measured on its own for Moonshine Small, Moonshine Medium, Nemotron and Parakeet, and the app now runs the same fixes for all of them, because every fix helped each model or did no harm. Whisper still skips the punctuation step, the question-mark fix and capitals, which it does well by itself.

## Setup and settings

- **Set up voice typing** offers Parakeet, Moonshine Small and Nemotron, with what each one is like to use.
- **The setup tutorial follows the voice model you import.** It explains why Nemotron and Parakeet also want the punctuation model. At the end it names the fixes that helped your model most in testing, and **Switch these on** turns on Numbers, Voice commands, Filler words and Emoji in one tap.
- Voice settings that don't apply to your voice model are greyed out, with a note saying why (for example, Moonshine's processing mode and cores, and Whisper's suppress-symbols option).

## Fixes

- A Nemotron or Parakeet dictation that stops early or loops keeps what it already heard.
- A pause just before a "?" or "." that Parakeet attaches to a word still counts as a pause, so voice commands and self-corrections said there work.
- Hold-to-talk ending while the voice window is open no longer restarts typing checks too early.

## Models

The new voice models are on the [models page](https://github.com/ColinCPittman/Pluto-Keyboard/releases/tag/models): `pluto-voice-nemotron-en.zip` and `pluto-voice-parakeet-110m-en.zip`. The punctuation and Smart corrections files haven't changed, so there's no need to download them again.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.5.x as usual (Obtainium picks it up). Your settings, learned words and imported models stay. The fixes that are off by default (Numbers, Voice commands, Filler words, Self-corrections, Quoted speech and Emoji) stay off until you switch them on. You can switch on the four the tests support most with the tutorial (Help → Show the setup tutorial again) or under Voice Input → Smart corrections.
