# Pluto Keyboard 0.6.1

Typing fixes now catch the slips you make when you go back and edit a message while distracted (a word left out, a word typed twice, a word left over from what you had before). And spoken years and dates come out as digits with every voice model.

## Typing fixes for editing slips

- **It reads your message as it is.** Smart corrections for typing used to check a sentence only as you finished typing it, and only the words before the cursor. Now it looks at the text in the box however it got there (typed, swiped, dictated, pasted, or a sentence you went back and changed), on both sides of the cursor. A sentence you're editing in the middle of is checked once you pause for a moment.
- **Fixes after the cursor.** A slip later in your text gets fixed without moving your cursor, so you can keep typing where you were. The fix shows up in the suggestion bar like any other, and one tap changes it back.
- **New kinds of fixes.**
  - A small word left out ("a cup coffee" → "a cup of coffee").
  - A word typed twice ("to to"), twice in two forms ("photos photo"), or left over from an edit.
  - Two words the wrong way round.
  - A word whose ending no longer fits ("a long lists" → "a long list", "you friends" → "your friends").
  - Two words that should be one, a word that should be two, or a missing apostrophe ("Every thing", "upto", "thats").
- **Two new switches** under Text Prediction → Smart corrections for typing. **Fix missing and extra words** and **Fix word endings** are on by default, but like the rest they only work when Smart corrections for typing itself is on. Joins, splits and apostrophes come with **Attempt to fix missed typos**.
- **Careful by design.** Edits are only tried around a word the model finds surprising, so clean sentences stay quick (about a quarter more checking time than 0.6.0 on the test PC). On every test set, including a set of real texts, none of the clean sentences were changed.
- **Left alone.** Text that was already in the box when you opened it (a quoted email, a signature, an old note) isn't touched until you change it, and neither are quoted mail lines starting with ">". Links, email addresses, @names, #tags and words with accents are never changed.
- **Undo sticks.** Changing a fix back (with the button, or by hand) keeps that fix from coming back, in any form.

## Spoken dates as digits

- "Twenty twenty one to twenty twenty four" becomes "2021-2024".
- "Eleven eleven twenty twenty six" becomes "11/11/2026".
- "November eleventh twenty twenty six" becomes "November 11th, 2026".
- It works the same with every voice model, and was tested on Moonshine Small and Medium, Nemotron and Parakeet.

## Numbers is on by default

**Numbers, times and amounts** is now on by default, so years and dates come out as digits without setting anything up. If you switched it off yourself, it stays off.

## Known limits

- A leftover word is the hardest slip to tell from a word you meant. It's only removed when the words after it make it clear, so many are still left for you to catch.
- About one wrong ending in three gets fixed. The rest are left as they are, rather than risk changing a word you meant.

## Models

No model has changed. Nothing new to download.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.6.0 as usual. Your settings, learned words and imported models stay.
